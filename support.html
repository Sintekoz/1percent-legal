Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the App and go to Settings > Delete Account. This will permanently delete your cloud data, authentication credentials, and locally stored habit entries. If the App asks you to log in again first, please log out, log back in, and then tap Delete Account.

You can also request account deletion by emailing us at iegorshelestov@gmail.com.

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap Continue with Email, enter your email address, then tap Forgot password?. You will receive a password-reset link by email.

Is my data backed up?

If you are signed in with Apple, Google, or email, your data is automatically synced to the cloud. If you use the App without an account, data is stored only on your device and is not backed up.

Can I use the App without creating an account?

Yes. Tap Continue without account on the login screen. Your data will be stored locally on your device only.
