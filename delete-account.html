1% Better – Daily Growth Tracker

If you want to delete your 1% Better account and associated cloud data, you can do it directly in the App by going to Settings > Delete Account.

If you cannot access the App or prefer to make a web request, use the email link below:

Request Account Deletion by Email

What will be deleted

  • Your account in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your cloud-stored habit data in Firebase Firestore.
  • Locally stored data on your current device when deletion is performed in the App.

What we may ask you to provide

To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm the email address or sign-in method associated with the account before processing the request. Do not send your password.

Response Time

We aim to respond to deletion requests within 7 days.
